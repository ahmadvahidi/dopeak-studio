DOPEAK Studio — Privacy Policy

DOPEAK Studio is an internal publishing tool used by the DOPEAK team to upload our own original videos to our own TikTok account. We do not collect, store or sell personal data of third parties.

The only data we handle is the OAuth access token issued by TikTok for our own account, stored on our own server and used exclusively to publish our videos. No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking of other users.

The token can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account settings, which immediately ends the tool's access.
