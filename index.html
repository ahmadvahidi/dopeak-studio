DOPEAK Studio

Internal publishing tool · DOPEAK

DOPEAK Studio is the private tool the DOPEAK team uses to publish our own original educational videos to our own TikTok account. It is not a public service, it has no third-party users, and it is not distributed.

The tool authenticates with TikTok through Login Kit, requests only video.publish and video.upload, and uploads content owned and produced by DOPEAK.
